# v0.0.29 Freeplay density checkpoint

Verified development checkpoint. The user subsequently authorized v0.0.29 publication; see [release validation](v0.0.29-release-validation.md). Future publication requires an explicit user request.

## Intended behavior

- Host chart cards keep their difficulty stars in a reserved right column. Only the selected card expands. Favorites stay first.
- Mouse hover and keyboard difficulty selection animate the same star with elapsed-time vertical motion and rotation. Extremely dense difficulties use a black star with a bounded rotating violet shadow. Star silhouettes have a subtle vertical gradient, a thicker dark outline and no rectangular backing. The extreme-tier violet glow keeps a fixed five-point star silhouette behind the black foreground star. A circulating gradient is baked once into a shared padded animation; only its edges are lightly softened, without a round halo or per-frame blur.
- Density is independent of completion and authored ratings. Average NPS counts player heads through the last charted note/hold endpoint, with a two-second minimum duration. Sustains count once, opponent and automatic extra fields do not count, and recognized avoid/hurt notes are separate. Shared source ownership rules remain authoritative.
- Peak NPS is the maximum player-head count in a half-open two-second window, divided by two. Danger frequency is dangers per charted second. A danger is in a fast passage when its centered two-second window contains at least 6 required player heads per second.
- Estimated difficulty is 0.6 * average NPS + 0.4 * peak NPS + min(3, 2 * danger frequency + 3 * fast-danger frequency). The stable continuous palette goes green, gold, orange, red, violet, then near-black. Only peak NPS >= 12 enables the black/shadow treatment. Values above the scale retain the extreme treatment and remain sortable without clipping the measured numbers.
- Unknown scripted note behavior is explicitly estimated; no donor scripts are executed by the analyzer and no chart/mod names are used as exceptions.
- The common import revision advances from 3 to 4. The analyzer version follows this revision, so an algorithm change must advance COMMON_REVISION. The existing retained refresher transactionally republishes per-difficulty chartDensity.json with the chart output. No audio decode is needed; fresh conversions reuse the already parsed chart. Base charts are analyzed once per named difficulty on demand.
- NPS difficulty sorting uses the independent named comparison difficulty, preserves favorites/group ordering, and leaves missing or unsupported measurements last.
- Misses, max combo and total required player heads are stored with the exact score record they describe, including recent, best score, best accuracy and best full combo. The synthesized best-overall record and legacy saves without statistics show unknown values. Donor-specific score stores are not rewritten.
- Ordinary Freeplay captions no longer prepend raw hexadecimal package badges. Full owner keys still govern saved metadata.

The final thicker-outline/star-shaped glow passed the same complete native checks at 60 FPS and unlimited FPS (tmp/v29-native-starhalo60.log and tmp/v29-native-starhalo0.log).
