The inspected Psych donor `source/states/PlayState.hx` calls stage creation at lines 464-465, then adds comboGroup/uiGroup/noteGroup at lines 476-478. The shared engine fix restores stage-created member order before the first native HUD member once after the Psych stage start and its existing refresh. It keeps stage members in their current relative order, does not rewrite any z values, and excludes HUD members themselves. Native/HXC stages retain their current path. Later song onCreate/onCreatePost and explicit object-order requests remain authoritative. No chart, mod script, media or installed playing-game files were changed.
