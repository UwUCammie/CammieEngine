# v0.0.41 release validation

This user-authorized incremental alpha fixes the multi-second freezes v0.0.40 introduced in Lua-heavy Psych songs, optimises script-heavy songs, makes cutscene skips land 5 seconds before play resumes, fixes the loud results screen count-up, and widens Psych, Nightmare Vision and V-Slice support at the engine, compatibility and importer level. Remaining roadmap requirements stay open.

## Build and regression evidence

The final Windows build completed successfully. Its executable SHA-256 is `34649d1bd35fbd07d1752e56caa50e459de45497a49088fc2b8fdd85243e5b44`.

The full suite executed 3,362 tests across 1,145 modules in 474 seconds with 347 fixture/platform skips and zero failed modules, through `run.bat package`, on the first attempt.

Before packaging, a whole-project type check passed and the focused tests for every changed file passed.

## Native checks

Muted runs on a private, never activated desktop against a private copy of a real install, with botplay and frame pacing telemetry, 90 seconds each:

- The 7 core songs (isotope, amusia, frostbite, left-unchecked, babybo, silly-billy and a Psych mod with a scripted intro) and 14 songs from example mods imported into the private copy (4 V-Slice, 10 Psych or Nightmare Vision): no crashes, no new script errors, no frame over 300 ms mid-song.
- isotope: v0.0.40 froze for 6 to 47 seconds at a time from about 29 s; now 0 frames over 300 ms, 5 collections instead of 18, worst frame about 130 ms.
- silly-billy: worst frame 102 ms to 64 ms, collections 14 to 9 per 90 s.
- A V-Slice song that spent close to a minute on the loading screen now loads in about 12 seconds.
- Cutscene skip: a mid-song skip landed 5 s before the next player note (6.1 s instead of 11.1 s); an intro video skip played the clip's last 5 s and the countdown followed on its own.
- Results screen: the count-up started 59 tick sounds on one frame before the fix and one per frame after it.

Not verified natively: loudness by ear (runs are muted), physical input, the Psych stage startVideo and plain intro video skip paths (covered by interpreter tests), and opening a Psych custom substate in game. Two song-end results screens still take one 200 to 340 ms frame to build.
