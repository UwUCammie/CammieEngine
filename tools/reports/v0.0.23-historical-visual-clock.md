## Scope and next connected work

This is the visual-transport prerequisite, not completion of historical sustain rendering. The renderer still uses modern endpoint stretching/orientation and clipping. Its historical endpoint is temporarily expressed in the new visual timeline; porting the historical future-clock/mAngle route will remove that per-note endpoint timeline lookup. Modern SV behavior remains explicitly unsupported pending its own source-version audit.

Next implement historical generation timestamps (base step plus step divided by songSpeed rounded to two decimals), post-skin raw sustain baselines, previous-frame mAngle consumption, the future visual-clock sample, and ReverseModifier clipping at its source callback position. Compare executable donor code, including getPos scratch-vector aliasing, rather than inferring orientation from geometric intent. Preserve modern sustain behavior. Pixel sheets, splash overloads, noteskin noteSplash/quants callbacks and the wider roadmap remain open.
