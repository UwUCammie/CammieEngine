# v0.0.38 release validation

This user-authorized incremental alpha reworks Freeplay presentation (card star field, rating colour key, block visualizer, popup library menus) and the loading screen (clean cutout, drop shadow, more stars), and adds a focused mode to the test runner. Remaining roadmap requirements stay open.

## Build and regression evidence

The final Windows build completed successfully. Its executable SHA-256 is `25b2a08e74570ad42d4d254a9ce6346b9d1060f88ca3b87b6dcfca4f9800ba7f`.

The full suite executed 2,898 tests across 982 modules in 454 seconds with 347 fixture/platform skips and zero failed modules, through `run.bat package`. During development one earlier full run had a single failed module, `test_import_io.py`; it passed alone and in the packaging run, and source files were being edited while that run was in progress.

## Freeplay

Unit checks cover the star field's motion, scatter, edge clipping and turn frames, the rating key's scale and markers, the block visualizer's colours, lit count and fall rate, and the popup layout.

Muted native framebuffers from a private desktop confirmed, on the build before the final packaging build of the same tree: stars entering from the right edge, cut by the top, bottom and right edges and gone before the middle of the card; the gradient key with numbers beneath it; compact stars without numbers; the block visualizer; and the F1 popup over the dimmed list with its chart card. The native probe also asserts that every visible star, after clipping, lies inside the card to within one pixel and that at least one star is cut by an edge; the average frame rate held at 60 with the field active.

The TAB popup and the menus opened from either popup share the same base class and were not captured. The visualizer was seen with a quiet preview only, so tall bars and their fall were not observed natively.

## Loading screen

Unit checks cover the cutout rule on synthetic pixels: backdrop removed, rim pixels unmixed, artwork and outlines kept, the feathered edge. A native capture of the loading screen showed clean edges; a scan of that capture for backdrop-tinted pixels found 8, in one sliver beside the speaker. The pale look of the two rear characters is how the source artwork is drawn. The violet drop shadow and the denser background stars were seen in the same capture.

## Test runner

`tools/run_tests.py --changed` selected 72 of 981 modules for the files changed in this release and ran them in about 159 seconds. Its selection rule has its own unit test. The default full run is unchanged and remains part of packaging.
