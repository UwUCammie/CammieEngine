# v0.0.45 release validation

This user-authorized incremental alpha lets mods that cannot be refreshed be removed from Freeplay, restores the health icons, hit splash position and dodge timing of V-Slice songs such as Expurgation at the engine and compatibility level, and stops Nightmare Vision songs such as Silly Billy from halting on a freed note splash sheet. Remaining roadmap requirements stay open.

## Build and regression evidence

The final Windows build completed successfully. Its executable SHA-256 is `8542b76571f60d23c42c1afbb236ee577d17659d8d9abbe1dcc0aaead0083770`.

The full suite executed 3,561 tests across 1,215 modules in 671 seconds with 347 fixture/platform skips and zero failed modules, through `run.bat package`. Before packaging, the tests for every changed file passed and a whole-project type check passed.

## Native checks

Muted runs on a private, never activated desktop against a private copy of a real install (the original was only read):

- Removing a mod whose refresh had failed (an older copy imported as the wrong engine, with its package folders already missing): the review used to stop at "active or reserved"; it now completes in 205 seconds, the linked copy goes with it, and Freeplay is back 0.4 seconds after Return.
- Expurgation (Tricky Clowned Out, V-Slice): the health icons are drawn over the bar art, hit splashes of the Tricky style land within 8 pixels of their receptor (before: about 50 pixels right and 100 pixels low), and a scripted space press at 22.8 s dodges the pillar while one at 22.55 s does not, matching the countdown's last beat.
- Silly Billy (Nightmare Vision) played to the end with two pause retries and no note splash errors.

Not verified natively: an intermittent access violation as the smoke process exits after a clean success (seen in about a third of runs that return to Freeplay, with and without a removal; not investigated in this release), and the dodge timing against a real keyboard.
