## Implementation

Historical owners use the source base-step hold spacing and first-tail offset divided by song speed rounded to two decimals. Post-skin construction uses actual atlas dimensions, converts previous caps into bodies and captures the raw scale through the shared field-scale helper. Modern NV generation and Psych's independent sustain path remain separate where their contracts differ.

The shared renderer consumes the previous mAngle during ConfusionModifier, then computes the next angle from the historical future visual clock. It preserves the source's reused versus replaced position-vector behavior. Historical holds keep their source vertical baseline and perspective projection; modern endpoint stretching remains unchanged. ReverseModifier performs source hit clipping at its position in the modifier chain, before later callbacks and scripted position offsets. Eligible clips replace the rectangle; ineligible clips retain the existing rectangle. Receptor eligibility is read from the live manager bank through the same helper used by direct script calls.

Historical XModifier now commits its speed setter exactly at the callback position. Snapshot flushes no longer invoke it again after clipping/custom callbacks or perspective, preventing the projected scale from overwriting the raw body baseline.

The existing shared note speed-resize path now admits generated historical NV holds and preserves their raw body baseline and cap behavior. No chart names, mod IDs, donor edits or asset substitutions were introduced.

## Scope and remaining work

This checkpoint does not complete Phase 2 or all sustain semantics. Pixel construction equations are donor-compared, but source pixel head/tail sheet loading and native pixel rendering remain open. Arbitrary constructor callbacks that change timing during generation, source-created/recycled notes, wider-key behavior and full authored SV scenes still require validation. Dynamic/fixed speed-target composition and precise raw songSpeed setter ordering remain explicit follow-up contracts. Do not infer their parity from normal four-key holds.

Keep historical noteskin noteSplash/quants callbacks, splash constructor/setup overloads, shader presets, remaining modifier/note-type behavior, advanced Animate and embedded/custom libraries open. Imported menu flows, mod management, categories/favorites, the dense Freeplay redesign, icon/star correctness, independent group/chart sorting and fidelity-preserving performance remain required.
