# v0.0.42 release validation

This user-authorized incremental alpha makes mod removal work for broken and linked imports, retries songs without the loading screen, stores Nightmare Vision base assets once across content packs, and widens Psych, Nightmare Vision and V-Slice support at the engine, compatibility and importer level. Remaining roadmap requirements stay open.

## Build and regression evidence

The final Windows build completed successfully. Its executable SHA-256 is `3da5301c241b9873b488ca9ab185e2ab0fc46424cb89db958013e5371837401e`.

The full suite executed 3,481 tests across 1,200 modules in 560 seconds with 347 fixture/platform skips and zero failed modules, through `run.bat package`.

The first three gate attempts failed only on timeouts while other applications loaded the machine. Every timed-out module passed on its own. Two interpreter fixtures (`test_import_removal_manager`, `test_import_io`) hang inside the Haxe interpreter's threads under concurrent load, and the v0.0.41 code hangs the same way. They now retry only on a timeout, never on a failed check. The other timeouts were native fixture builds that pass alone in 6 to 106 seconds.

Before packaging, a whole-project type check passed and the focused tests for every changed file passed.

## Native checks

Muted runs on a private, never activated desktop against a private copy of a real install, with botplay and frame pacing telemetry, 90 seconds each:

- 27 songs across Psych, Nightmare Vision and V-Slice mods: no crashes, and no frame over 300 ms that came from the game (one 437 ms frame was a 397 ms stall outside the process).
- Retry after a game over or from the pause menu: no loading screen after the first load, with the same notes, scripts and images kept. A Psych retry went from about 8.2 s to about 1 to 3 s.
- A multi-pack Nightmare Vision mod went from about 8.7 GB to about 0.5 GB on disk, and its re-import took about 4 minutes instead of 13. Its songs still play to the end.
- Removal: rollback, retry, non-ASCII registries and missing record pointers are covered by the removal tests; the held-session dialog is covered by the removal UI test.

Not verified natively: loudness by ear (runs are muted), physical input, and the look of the Nightmare Vision pack songs (checked through success, problem lines and frame telemetry). Retrying a song still keeps two small score text graphics per retry.
