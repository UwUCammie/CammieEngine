CammieEngine update log

v0.0.47 alpha - CammieEngine
- Online play: race other players on the same song in lobbies of 2 to 16
- Sign in with your uwucammie.com account
- Songs you don't have are sent by the host and kept in a separate cache
- Scores are checked by the server, with match history and song leaderboards
- New scoring: 1,000,000 for a perfect full combo, with SS to D ranks
- Your old scores were converted to the new scale
- New results screen that opens instantly
- Modifiers are now a panel in Freeplay and Story mode (F2)
- Removed the Slow, Fast and Accel modifiers
- Character select moved to F3
- Signing in on the website no longer signs you out everywhere else

v0.0.46 alpha - CammieEngine
- Downloads and updates now come from https://uwucammie.com/cammie-engine/ instead of GitHub
- Updates only download the files that changed, and every file is checked before it is installed
- Your settings, mods and edited files are kept when updating

v0.0.45 alpha - CammieEngine
- Fixed mods that could not be removed after an update
- Fixed health icons hidden under the health bar in some V-Slice songs, such as Expurgation
- Fixed V-Slice note splashes appearing away from the receptor
- Fixed V-Slice events firing a beat early, which broke Expurgation's dodge timing
- Fixed Nightmare Vision songs such as Silly Billy stopping on a missing note splash animation

v0.0.44 alpha - CammieEngine
- New title screen logo with a neon outline and a trans flag colour shine sweeping across it
- Import moved from Options to the main menu
- New look for the options menu, main menu and importer, matching Freeplay
- Fixed many imported V-Slice songs, including Tricky's Expurgation, Satin Panties (Sonic Mix) and Windbreak: stages, characters, props, vocals, receptors and the countdown
- Fixed Psych characters with a space in their name and add-on songs that use the main mod's characters and icons
- The camera in Psych and Nightmare Vision songs now moves at the same speed as in those engines
- Fixed imports getting stuck on "Another import refresh is already running" after moving the game folder
- The importer now says why it cannot start

v0.0.43 alpha - CammieEngine
- Fixed a blank screen when removing a mod while mods were still being refreshed

v0.0.42 alpha - CammieEngine
- Removing a mod is more reliable and can close running mod menus for you when they are in the way
- Fixed removals failing when a song list contained non-English text
- Retrying a song after a death or from the pause menu is now almost instant
- Psych songs load much faster (frostbite in 5 seconds instead of 12)
- Many more V-Slice scripts, characters and menus work, including Animate atlas characters and custom song menus
- Shorter pauses for memory cleanup during songs
- Large Nightmare Vision mods use far less disk space (one went from 8.7 GB to 0.5 GB) and re-import much faster
